Data source

Built on the paperwork every US import leaves behind.

  1. US Customs bills of ladingPublic sea-shipment records: importer, product, containers and arrival date.

  2. Matched to what you makeFiltered by your HS codes and product descriptions, then ranked by fit.

  3. Historical, not liveRecords describe arrivals that already happened. Sea lanes are indicative, never a vessel track.

How the headline number is worked out

Where comes from

  • Import values are estimates. Each importer's yearly figure is estimated from their customs records. On this site it comes from the demo dataset.
  • It assumes every purchase goes through a buying agent at 5%. Real agent rates vary (often 3–8%), and some importers buy direct already.
  • It is the commission pool, not your income. It sizes what agents take on this trade each year. What you win depends on price, quality and the deals you close.
  • ₹95.00 to US$1, the fixed rate the app uses for every rupee figure.
